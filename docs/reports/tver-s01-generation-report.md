**Prepared:** 3 July 2026

> ⚠️ **Data basis — read before use.** The daily generation figures in this report are **modelled estimates** derived from each site's installed DC capacity and a seasonal Thailand solar-yield curve (≈4.2 peak-sun-hours, 0.8 performance ratio). They are **not** revenue-meter readings. Before submitting this report to a funder or auditor, replace the modelled generation with **actual metered data** for each site. Emission reductions are computed exactly as the platform computes them: `reduction = Σ(daily_kWh × 0.51) ÷ 1000` tCO₂e.

## 4. Method & assumptions

- **Registration.** All 19 sites are registered under **T-VER-S-01** (grid-connected solar PV, T-VER v3.0) with a Project Design Document per site.
- **Baseline.** Grid electricity displaced by on-site solar generation.
- **Emission factor.** Thailand grid factor **0.51 kgCO₂e/kWh** (EGAT/TGO), selected automatically by the platform from the site's country and the record date.
- **Formula (grid displacement).** For each day, `reduction_kgCO₂e = generation_kWh × 0.51`; summed over the period and converted to tCO₂e (÷1000). Values are rounded to 3 decimals per day, matching the platform's calculation engine.
- **Modelled generation.** `daily_kWh ≈ capacity_kWp × 4.2 peak-sun-hours × 0.8 performance-ratio × seasonal × daily-variation`. **Placeholder for real meter data.**
- **Annualisation.** Period tCO₂e × (365 ÷ 212). Indicative only.
